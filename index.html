      Phase 3: The Waypoint Ambush

      Do not push through the "dead space" between decimals. Holster your inputs and let the public pool (other players or natural networked volume) push the ticker toward the nearest execution node.

      Phase 4: The 10-Tick Snipe

      When the ticker crosses a decimal node (e.g., hits .50 or .70), inject your capital for exactly 10 ticks (a $20 volume test). If nothing hits, stop immediately and wait for the next node. If a feature hits, let it play out completely.
